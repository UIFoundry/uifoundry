## Design Notes

This testimonial block features:

- **Single centered focus**: Displays one powerful testimonial at a time for maximum impact
- **Large quote typography**: Prominent 2xl font size with italic styling and relaxed leading
- **Professional avatar display**: 2xl avatar with primary-colored ring and offset for visual depth
- **Clean author attribution**: Centered card header with name and role clearly separated
- **Responsive padding**: Scales from py-6 on mobile to py-16 on larger screens
- **Maximum width constraint**: Content limited to xl (36rem) for optimal readability
- **Accessible semantics**: Includes screen-reader-only "Testimonials" heading

Use this component for featuring high-value testimonials from key customers, executives, or industry leaders. Perfect for landing pages, about pages, or case studies where you want to highlight a single powerful endorsement.
